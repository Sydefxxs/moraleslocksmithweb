Questions

Something else? Call or text (956) 455-9249.

Do you come to me?

Yes. Morales Locksmith is mobile. Felipe drives to your car, home or business anywhere in Brownsville, South Padre Island and the Rio Grande Valley.

I lost all my car keys. Can you make a new one?

Yes. A new key can be cut and programmed at the car, so you don't need to tow it to the dealer.

How much does it cost?

It depends on the car and the key. Call or text the year, make and model (a photo of the key helps) and you'll get a price before anyone drives out.

Can you program a key fob or push-to-start key?

Yes. New and extra fobs and smart keys are programmed on the spot for most makes, including Ford, Chevrolet, Dodge, Chrysler, Jeep, Toyota, Honda and Nissan.

Are you open on weekends?

Some Saturdays and Sundays. Call ahead to check.
